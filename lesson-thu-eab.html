Emerald Ash Borer

⚠️

Regulated pest — BC under active surveillance. EAB has established in Alberta (confirmed 2019, Edmonton area) and is spreading west. BC is not yet confirmed infested (as of 2026) but threat is imminent. Every green ash in the Okanagan is at risk. Know this pest before it arrives.

📋 EAB at a Glance

Scientific name
Agrilus planipennis Fairmaire (Coleoptera: Buprestidae)
Origin
Asia (China, Korea, Russia, Japan); accidentally introduced to North America ~1990s
First detected NA
Detroit/Windsor area, 2002
Host range
All Fraxinus spp. (ash); no other confirmed hosts
Kill rate
Nearly 100% of untreated ash within 3–5 years of infestation
Diagnostic sign
D-shaped exit holes (3–4 mm); serpentine galleries under bark; crown dieback top-down
Spread vector
Primarily firewood movement; adult flight 10–20 km/year natural spread
BC status (2026)
Not confirmed in BC; active trapping surveillance program; CFIA regulated area covers much of Alberta
