Quick Reference — The Water Potential Equation

Water Potential (ψ)
ψ = ψs + ψp + ψg
Solute + Pressure + Gravity
Direction of Flow
High ψ → Low ψ
Soil → Root → Stem → Leaf → Air
Driving Force
Transpiration pull
Evaporation at stomata creates tension
Enabling Property
Cohesion + Adhesion
H-bonds between water molecules
Failure Mode
Cavitation / embolism
Air bubble breaks water column
Repair Strategy
Refilling at night
Root pressure + osmotic recovery
