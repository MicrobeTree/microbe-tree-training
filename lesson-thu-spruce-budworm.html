🦠 Western Spruce Budworm

Choristoneura freemani (formerly C. occidentalis) · The active BC Interior outbreak you're driving through every day

Life Cycle — Quick Reference

🥚
July–August: Eggs laid on needle undersides in masses of ~25. Hatch in ~10 days. L1 larvae spin hibernaculum (silk shelter) on bark and enter dormancy.
😴
September–April: 2nd instar larvae (L2) overwinter under bark scales. Do not feed during this period.
🌱
Late April–May: L2 larvae emerge, mine into developing buds, feed on expanding new growth. This is the critical damage period.
🟤
June–July: Late instar larvae consume current-year needles and strobili. Silk webbing and frass visible. Brown tips appear on Douglas-fir throughout the crown.
🦋
Late July: Adults (rusty-brown moths, 20–25 mm wingspan) emerge, mate, lay eggs. One generation per year.
