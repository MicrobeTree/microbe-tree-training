Water Potential, Osmosis & Drought Stress

📋 Water Potential at a Glance

Ψ (water potential) = Ψs (solute) + Ψp (pressure)
Water potential (Ψ)
Measure of free energy of water; always flows high → low (0 to negative)
Pure water reference
Ψ = 0 MPa (megapascals). All plant cells have negative Ψ
Solute potential (Ψs)
Always negative; more solutes = more negative Ψs = more water uptake
Pressure potential (Ψp)
Turgor pressure in living cells; positive in turgid cells, zero at wilting
Osmosis
Water moves across semipermeable membrane from high Ψ to low Ψ
Cohesion-tension
Transpiration creates negative Ψ in leaves → water pulled up from roots
Drought effect
Soil Ψ becomes more negative → reduces gradient → water uptake slows
Field relevance
Explains wilting, embolism, stomatal closure, and irrigation timing
